                        Starting a Business

                        Your launch roadmap

                        Follow these steps to build a strong, secure foundation from day one.

                        1. Define your business ideaIdentify your product or service, research your target market, and size up competitors.
                        2. Write a business planExecutive summary, market analysis, pricing, and 12-month financial projections.
                        3. Choose a business structureSole proprietorship, LLC, or Corporation — each has different liability and tax implications.
                        4. Register your businessRegister a business name with your state or county clerk's office.
                        5. Get an EIN (Tax ID)Apply free at IRS.gov. Required for taxes, banking, and hiring.
                        6. Open a business bank accountKeep personal and business finances separate from day one.
                        7. Set up basic cybersecurityEnable MFA, use a password manager, and schedule automatic data backups.
                        8. Build your online presenceRegister a domain, create a Google Business Profile, and set up social media.
                        9. Get required licenses & permitsCheck local business license rules and any industry-specific permits.
                        10. Set up bookkeepingTrack income and expenses from day one. Consider Wave (free) or QuickBooks.
